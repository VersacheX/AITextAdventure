OVERWORLD MAP
-------------
Use arrow keys to move
press i to open inventory
press q to quit

# Map symbols
µ - The Bar (found in every city and region)
@ - The Inn (found in every city)
Æ - The Weapons Shop (found in every city)
₨ - The Items Shop (found in every city)
¥ - The Armor Shop (found in every city)
Î î Ï ï - Large/Small Residences and Commercial Buildings.  These are great places to loot and stay safe.
Ṣ - The Hyperway (used to fast travel to any known city)
O - City Specific Building or location
0 - City Specific Building or location
Ð - Dungeon Entrance

*anything else is either open area or an impassable object

INVENTORY
---------
use arrow keys left and right to select characters
press e to equip
 - you can filter equipment type using -/+
 - select the items to equip and press enter

# LEVELING
your starting character will have Unused Awards
'Pow: 15 | Stat: 10 | Abil: 1'
press u to upgrade stats
you start with 15 pow pts and 10 stat

# power points
HP - Health, sustainable damage
AP - Ability Points

# stat points
str - melee damage. primary stat for technique 
dex - evasion, accuracy, combat speed. primary stat for skill, secondary for tech
int - pure ability effect. primary stat for tech, magic, and spirit
con - health buff, resistance. secondary stat for technique

press l to learn abilities
# LEARN ABILITIES
requirements for level 1
technique - 13 str/ 9 con
tech - 13 int/ 9 dex
spirit - 13 int/ 9 con
magic - 18 int
skill - 18 dex
ability level requirements scale, so be sure to spend wisely and focus

press i to use items, press -/+ to filter between
press enter to use an item

press n to view the npc log
cycle through them with up and down arrows.  if an npc has a * they have a task waiting

press m to review all hostiles you've encountered

DUNGEONS
--------
move with arrow keys
↑↓ when on an arrow press u or d to change floor
◘ are treasures, press space to loot
ß are hostiles and npcs, press space to interact

SAVING
------
You can save your game using a slot name.
Later you can reload from any slot name.
when you save without entering a slot name it overwrites that slots data

PARTY
-----
In the inventory screen press p to select party members.  you can have any amount from 1-5
