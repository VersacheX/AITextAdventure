###################THE ENDINGS
In edit the final 5 endings will not be about choice, but about player actions.

actual endings:
1. You have failed - failing to recruit all 16 mbti story characters before defeating the void leaves not enough humanity for existence to survive.  The player loses and annihilation happens
2. This is minimal - defeat void after recruiting the 16 mbti story characters.  The minimum of humanity has been saved.  catastrophe still happens, but humanity survives to rebuild
3. The party ending - defeat the void with 16 mbti story characters saved fighting the void with a party comprised of Chock, Moxie, Kade, Kaera, and Poise (the main characters).  The party returns to their home world back at the bar and celebrate victory and libations.
4. The philosophical dream ending - recruit all 16 mbti story characters, then defeat the void with a party comprised solely of the players custom character.  Players character wakes up, it was all a dream.
5. The ultimate ending - defeat the void after recruiting all 16 mbti story characters and all secret sidequest characters (not created yet, but there will be 32 more characters to find in the city sidequests unrelated to the main story).  In this ending humanity wins, collapse does not happen.

possible concept of 16 additional endings: (prerequisite, must have recruited the original 16 mbti story characters)
each of these concept endings involves defeating the void with a party of 3 characters of any of the mbti types
